## Additional RGE visual review

- Tracksmith, [Welcome To The Team](https://reallygoodemails.com/emails/welcome-to-the-team): reviewed the complete visual. Studied large contextual photography, restrained brand palette, breathing room and alternating image modules.
- Flamingo Estate, [Tomato, Tomato, Tomato](https://reallygoodemails.com/emails/tomato-tomato-tomato): reviewed the complete visual. Studied a direct hero CTA, strong type contrast, section color reversals and consistent product storytelling.

Applied principles, not copied layouts or assets: moved Cedar Street’s primary action into the opening module; retained a photographic hero, editorial welcome and contrasting repair feature. Added a short design rationale and measurement plan to each concept. Reference imagery is not shipped with the site.

## Example brand fonts

Font files and their SIL Open Font Licenses are in `public/fonts/examples/`. Local font faces use swap and system fallbacks.
